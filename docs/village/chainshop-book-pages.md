## Identity and evidence safeguards

Page 5 explicitly says the 1851 census used schedule numbers and that house renumbering probably happened several times. Its William Kendrick is at No. 7 in 1871; the later workshop holding is No. 14. Never automatically replace the household identity attached to a current map number using these numbers.

The author explicitly says there is no proof anyone lived in the attached cottage. Do not furnish every workshop annex as a confirmed dwelling.

The 1927 conveyance describes a house, chainshops and outbuildings on about 35 perches, but gives no building footprints. The mineral-rights clause permits removal without a guarantee of support; it does not prove actual subsidence damage at this property.

A Wesleyan Methodist chapel is documented through an 1829 service reference. It is a useful research lead, but its site and survival in 1865 remain unverified.
