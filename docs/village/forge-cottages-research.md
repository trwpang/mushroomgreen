# Forge Cottages: evidence check, 20 September 2026

## Question

Tom identified the modern Forge Cottages 21–29 range in Google Street View. These modern addresses are separate from the historic household IDs used by the village. Do not renumber the dataset.

## Direct observation

The user-selected August 2025 panorama is labelled 25 Mushroom Grn. It shows a low brick range, a clay-tile pitched roof, a substantial brick chimney, two louvred ridge structures, and a gated passage bearing a Forge Cottages sign. The roof structures appear to be ventilators. This supports a workshop interpretation, but does not establish the date or original purpose of each feature. Modern repairs and conversion can preserve or reproduce industrial details.

## Records

1. Dudley Council, *Mushroom Green Conservation Area, Planning Guidance Note 22*, approved 25 July 1996, p. 1: identifies a Griff Chains chainshop established in 1865. It also describes the separate preserved chainshop and the changing building stock. This is not an explicit construction date for the modern Forge Cottages range.
   Source: https://www.dudley.gov.uk/media/3xcfak1v/mushroom-green-guidance-note-22.pdf
2. Association for Industrial Archaeology, *Dudley Tour Notes*, September 1991, PDF p. 7: identifies John Griffiths as the hamlet's factor, links his works to Griff Chains Ltd, and records that parts of the original works survived as garages. It also mentions the preserved six-hearth shop. This gives a strong lead for industrial reuse, but does not name Forge Cottages or locate those garages precisely.
   Source: https://industrial-archaeology.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/1991-AIA-Dudley-Tour-Notes.pdf
3. Council note pp. 2–3: inspected the historical maps (1787, 1819, uncertain mid-nineteenth-century map, 1903, 1939) and the conservation-area map. These small reproductions do not support a confident parcel-level match or pre-1865 date for the exact Street View range.

## Current assessment

- A former workshop complex is a plausible interpretation of the visible building.
- A connection with the old Griff works is a research lead, not an established identification.
- No reliable construction or residential-conversion date for this exact range was found.
- Do not transfer the 1865 establishment date to every surviving wall, chimney or roof vent.
- Do not confuse the council's demolished/rebuilt platelayer's hut with demolition of the chain works.
- Do not confuse Griff Chains with Griffin-Woodhouse, another business in the AIA tour notes.

## Consequence for the model

Keep the historic map and household numbering authoritative. Do not insert the present-day complex wholesale into the 1865 scene from its appearance alone. A planning conversion drawing with an existing-site plan, or a clearer dated large-scale map, is the next useful source for matching this footprint. Current workshop forms can inform modelling details while their exact date remains unproved. No scene changes were made during this research check.
