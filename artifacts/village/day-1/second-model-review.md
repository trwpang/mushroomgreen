- The approach view works as the cover image. The chainshop holds the centre. The warm open entrance forms the main focal point.
- A muddy arrival track bends toward the entrance. A dark foreground puddle adds depth and a visible reflection.
- Damp earth, scattered stones, coal, timber, barrels, salvaged brick, benches, threshold flags, and worn roof material show a used working yard.
- The light stays low and quiet. Warm shop light contrasts with the subdued ground and vegetation.
- Smoke, pale trees, and distant cottages form clear atmosphere layers behind the shop.
- The entrance view shows brickwork, patched slate, shutters, three chimneys, stored timber, coal, and the lit threshold at useful inspection scale.
- The lane view shows the settlement layout, connected tracks, and distance haze.
- The map overlay gives a readable north-up site view with all 59 numbered household plots.
- The clean-interface approach frame is suitable for a 16:9 exported image.
